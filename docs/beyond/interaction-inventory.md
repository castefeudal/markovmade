Only the motion presentation module owns the settling scheduler. It reads cached geometry on intent or invalidation, settles to zero RAF work, pauses when hidden, and disables physics for coarse pointers, reduced motion, Save-Data and low-memory devices. No per-element perpetual loops were added. The real portrait/head-tracking module retains its original owner.
