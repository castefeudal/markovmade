# LAB PRO methods and compatibility audit

Reviewed 2026-11-01. This is a source and arithmetic audit, not clinical or prospective validation. Browser fixtures are synthetic QA inputs, never preloaded personal observations.

| Tool | What the number means | Assumptions and verification |
|---|---|---|
| Body | BF is entered or estimated by the circumference equation. FM = mass × BF/100; FFM = mass − FM; FFMI = FFM / height². | Numeric male/female Navy fixtures and unit conversion are tested. No direct muscle measurement. The user may supply ± percentage points: the same BF endpoints propagate to FM/FFM/FFMI and constant-FFM target weight. These are scenarios, not statistical intervals. Kouri height correction is shown for men only; it is not a genetic or drug-use test. Invalid circumference estimates are rejected rather than silently clamped. |
| Calories | Resting-energy prediction, activity assumption, observed maintenance estimate and chosen calorie plan are different quantities. | Mifflin and ten Haaf fixtures; 14–28-day calibration, missing adherence, water noise and energy-equivalent sensitivity tests. No double counting PAL with detailed activity. Protein/fat must fit the selected calorie plan; an impossible scenario returns an error. Model margins are chosen planning margins. |
| Overeating | A conditional energy-equivalent range, separate from glycogen-associated water. | Gross surplus, macro-weighted TEF and water bounds have deterministic fixtures. Storage/TEF factors and 7700 kcal/kg are scenario assumptions, not a tissue measurement or probability model. Gut contents, sodium and other fluid changes remain unestimated. Component scenarios must not be added as measured scale-weight fractions. |
| Recovery | A transparent weighted self-report heuristic; RHR/HRV and sleep plots are entered observations. | Baseline uses ≥14 distinct paired dates in a 28-calendar-day window, with an exact device/protocol label match. Duplicate/invalid dates and stale windows are tested. No pooling across labels. An absent label is unverified, not proof of device consistency. The score and its thresholds are not validated diagnostic or injury-risk predictions. |
| Progress | Actual dated weights with a Theil–Sen line and residual-MAD review flags. | Tests cover irregular spacing, 7/14/28-day windows, duplicates, impossible dates, retained outliers, insufficient observations and missing adherence. ≥7 observed dates spanning ≥6 days are required for the new trend. No filling gaps. The window ends at the latest weight record and its dates are displayed. The older manually entered daily series remains separately identified. ETA is a conditional linear scenario, not a promised date. |
| Strength | Epley/Brzycki e1RM reference, stored exercise history and rounded reference loads. | Numeric fixtures and rep bounds. A single repetition does not prove a maximal attempt. Optional RIR/protocol are stored; unknown conditions remain unknown. Matching labels do not verify real technique. No maximal-load testing instruction or automatic load increase follows from e1RM. |
| Plates | Exact bounded inventory arithmetic, including bar and total collar mass. | Integer grams; up to 24 plates per side; nearest attainable load below target and separate higher comparison; minimum plate count per sum. 120 seeded random inventories are compared against exhaustive subsets, including microplates, duplicates, empty stock and unreachable targets. No reusing plates. Physical calibration and collar distribution must be checked by the user. |
| Progression | An explicit MARKOVMADE heuristic over entered training sessions. | Compare the last two sessions: exercise, load, sets, technique label, range label and rest must match. Both need the rep ceiling and minimum RIR ≥2; available increment ≤10%. Each mismatch has a tested reason. These chosen thresholds are not a validated guarantee of readiness or safety. Quick mode remains available; PRO rows replace its load/rep/RIR inputs. |
| Caffeine | Sum of entered doses under hypothetical first-order half-lives of 3/5/7 hours. | Sum and clock/midnight fixtures; each dose contributes only after its entered time. Clock times refer to the preceding 24 hours before bedtime. The comparison moves the latest dose up to two hours earlier within that window. No blood concentration or sleep-quality forecast. Input bounds are validation bounds, not recommended doses. EFSA's general adult reference is not an individual safe dose. |

## Sources and scope

- [Mifflin et al., 1990](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2305711/): resting-energy prediction, not measured daily expenditure. The original abstract describes healthy adults; applicability outside the study population is not established by this implementation.
- [Kouri et al., 1995](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/7496846/): a preliminary male-athlete study. Its sample does not justify universal natural-potential thresholds or individual drug-use classification.
- Hodgdon–Beckett Navy reports 84-11/84-29: original circumference model references retained in the tool. Group validation error is not a personal confidence interval. This audit does not independently validate the Navy equations on a new population.
- [Hall et al., 2011](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21872751/): dynamic body-weight modelling. The short overeating tool is explicitly a simpler scenario and is not presented as an implementation of Hall's dynamic model.
- [Brzycki, 1993](https://doi.org/10.1080/07303084.1993.10606684): repetition-based strength estimation. Exercise, technique, fatigue and proximity to failure limit individual accuracy.
- [Sen, 1968](https://doi.org/10.1080/01621459.1968.10480934): robust slope estimator. The minimum observations, plateau/ETA rules and adjustment thresholds are product heuristics, not conclusions of that paper.
- [HRV Task Force, 1996](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8838590/): measurement and interpretation reference. The product's 14-day median and score weights are separate heuristics; the paper does not validate this score.
- [EFSA caffeine assessment](https://www.efsa.europa.eu/en/topics/topic/caffeine), [Drake et al., 2013](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3805807/): general caffeine evidence. The three chosen half-lives are illustrative assumptions, not a personalised pharmacokinetic fit.
- Embedded protein, load, tonnage, phase, circumference, recomposition, sleep and fatigue scenarios keep their own formula/evidence disclosures. Exact set/time/mass arithmetic is distinguished from practical thresholds and biological estimates.

## Storage, versions and rounding

No existing local-storage key is removed. Body/recovery and new strength records identify 1.1.0; nutrition/progress/overeating retain their existing equation version. New dated analyses identify their own 1.0.0 helper version. Plates/caffeine are 2.3.0; progression is 2.2.0. Requested archived plates/caffeine 2.0.0 and 2.2.0 and progression 2.0.0/2.1.0 use captured prior runners. Old cached outcomes are displayed with their stored version rather than relabelled current.

Calculations keep full precision except the exact plate solver's integer grams and its displayed 0.001 kg totals. Display rounding is not statistical precision. Missing data are not treated as zero observations, known adherence, verified technique or normal physiology. Dates are ISO calendar dates; daily deduplication keeps the last entered record for that date. Charts show measured/entered points separately from derived lines; decorative scenes contain no fictional trend.
